# Stitch prompts used for the M9 games addendum

Project `10251972321848422943` ("Sprout"), device MOBILE, model default (Gemini 3.8 Flash). Every call timed out client-side after about a minute; the screens landed 3 to 6 minutes later and were visible in `get_project` (`screenInstances`) first. Five of the twelve came back on a 2560 by 2048 desktop canvas despite `deviceType: MOBILE`; the PNGs in `designs/` are cropped to the phone. Downloading twelve files in one parallel batch collided on the tool-result file names; download one at a time.

- **Memory, showing:** "1 of 8"; "Watch carefully!"; white sequence card with four 52px slots, slot 1 blue square, slot 2 red circle, 3 and 4 empty; four 72px choice tiles (yellow square, blue square, red circle, green square) dimmed and disabled; caption "Showing the order".

Then the same content as A, restyled: segmented progress of fat rounded segments, a round sky countdown ring with the seconds inside, marigold answer tiles with dark bottom edges, a "Streak 3" line with a flat flame icon, sequence slots on a lighter green panel, a marigold rosette on the result, a lime reward card, "Done" as a marigold tile.
