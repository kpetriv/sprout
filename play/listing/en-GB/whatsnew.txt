Restore your account on a new or wiped phone. If the app's data was cleared, the app was reinstalled or the phone was replaced, sign in with the same Google account and enter your parent PIN: the household comes back and the old phone is removed. Forgot the PIN? Ask on the new phone and you can restore seven days later.

After updating, open the parent area with your PIN once so the restore is ready. Settings, Parent security shows the state.
