# M9: Visual redesign ("Sunny garden")

A visual redesign of every screen, shipped as 1.1.0 with new screenshot goldens and new store listing images, plus one structural change Kiril asked for on 2026-11-11: a bottom navigation bar for the parent and kid shells (§5). No new features beyond that, no data or sync changes. Base: `main` after the M8 release work has merged; nothing in M9 touches the release branch or the 1.0.0 build on the internal track.

The direction was brainstormed with Google Stitch (project "Sprout", `https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/10251972321848422943`, owned by Kiril) and chosen by Kiril on 2026-11-11 after seeing three rendered directions (D-M9-1). Stitch renders are the visual reference; they are HTML, not Compose, so implementers translate them into `core/designsystem` tokens and components. Local copies of every render used for a decision live in `.stitch/designs/`; `.stitch/metadata.json` maps them to Stitch screen ids.

Kiril chose **B** for its playfulness, with four changes that define the rest of this milestone:

1. **Palette:** the primary-school green/yellow/blue reads as off. Keep green as the brand colour; build a more sophisticated, harmonious set of soft garden tints around it.
2. **Type:** Nunito is not it. A characterful display face for titles and numbers, a clean readable sans for everything else.
3. **Components:** streamline for a slicker, more eye-pleasing feel: one card style, one icon style, consistent radius and spacing, no shadows, gradients or emoji.
4. **A colour dial by age:** Seedling gets the full garden, Sprout gets colour only where it means something, Sapling is as restrained as the parent side and matches it.

Stitch then refined B on its own brief (Kiril: "let Stitch do the brainstorming"): three `generate_variants` runs of the wallet and the dashboard plus three "refined take" generations and one in-place edit, ten renders in all (`.stitch/designs/B1-*.png`, `sheet-wallets-1.png`, `sheet-wallets-2.png`, `sheet-dashboards.png`). Rules that hold regardless of value: text on any tint keeps 4.5:1 (Honey `#C4922A` on white is 3.2:1, so it is icon and large-text only); state is never colour alone; the lavender Material defaults that leak into 1.0's `surfaceVariant` cards disappear because every container role is set explicitly; dynamic colour stays optional and off; nothing is pure black.

### 2.2 Typography

- **Display face** for titles, screen names and every number that matters (balances, counts, minutes): **Outfit**, 800 for hero amounts, 700 for headlines and row amounts, tight tracking, tabular figures (`FontFeatureSettings "tnum"`) wherever amounts align in a list.
- **Text face** for body, labels, buttons and rows: **Plus Jakarta Sans**, 500 for body, 600 for body-large, 700 for labels and button text.
- Both are OFL and bundled as `res/font` in `core/designsystem` (D-M9-4): offline, deterministic in Robolectric, no downloadable-font plumbing. Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic subsets; the build fails if `£` or Cyrillic glyphs are missing (a unit test renders a sample string through `Paint.hasGlyph`).
- `Typography` mapping: `displayLarge/Medium/Small`, `headlineLarge/Medium/Small`, `titleLarge` → display face; `titleMedium/Small`, `bodyLarge/Medium/Small`, `labelLarge/Medium/Small` → text face. Sizes stay Material's; `Typography.scaledBy(tier.typeScale)` keeps working unchanged. Sentence case everywhere; no all-caps headlines.
- Per tier: type scale unchanged (1.3 / 1.15 / 1.0). Body never below 14 sp, kid tiers never below 16 sp after scaling. The font-scale 2.0 goldens stay in the matrix.

### 2.3 Shape

`TierTokens.cornerRadius` becomes 28 / 24 / 18 dp (Seedling / Sprout / Sapling and parent; was 28 / 20 / 12), the card radii of the chosen renders. The five Material slots keep their factors (0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2), so `TierShapesTest` still holds. Buttons are pills (`CircleShape`) at every tier; cards and tiles use the medium slot.

### 2.4 Spacing and layout

A `SproutSpacing` object in `core/designsystem`: 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 dp steps, gutter 20 dp (was 16), card padding 20 dp, 8 dp between rows, 24 dp between sections. Content width stays capped at 640 dp with gutters that scroll with the content (D-V1-22). Tablet list-detail layouts keep their structure and only take the new tokens.

### 2.5 Motion

Buttons scale to 0.96 on press with a gentle spring; cards do not move. Confetti (`core/ui` `Confetti`) uses Botanical, Honey and Sky. The time bank ring sweeps as today. Everything honours reduced motion (`LocalReducedMotion` already exists from M7).

### 2.6 Per-tier scaling: the colour dial

`TierTokens` gains a fourth field, `colourLevel: ColourLevel`, with `FULL` (Seedling), `ACCENTED` (Sprout) and `RESTRAINED` (Sapling and the parent side). `TierTokensTest.youngerTiersGetLargerValues` grows an assertion that the level is monotonic too.

| Level | Who | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| `FULL` | Seedling (5 to 7) | Big blocks of Leaf, Honey and Sky tint; pictorial components (coin jar, sun, five stars); one or two giant pill buttons per screen; pictogram icons larger than the text |
| `ACCENTED` | Sprout (8 to 11) | Tints only where they carry meaning: Leaf-tint chip on the money hero, Honey screen-time badge and Shop pill, Sky goal icon; everything else white cards on cream |
| `RESTRAINED` | Sapling (12+) and parents | Cream and white, Botanical as the only accent, Honey and Sky reserved for figures and dots. A Sapling screen and a parent screen look like the same app; parents additionally get Honey-tint number circles on attention rows |

Components read the level instead of the tier: `SproutCard(meaning = CardMeaning.Money)` resolves to Leaf tint under `FULL`, to a white card with a Leaf-tint chip under `ACCENTED` and to a plain white card under `RESTRAINED`. Screens keep switching *content* on `KidUiTier` as today; they stop choosing colours themselves.

### 2.7 Icons and pictograms

Emoji go (D-M9-5). Controls use Material Symbols Outlined at weight 500 (vector drawables in `core/designsystem`), filled only for an active state, as in the renders. Chore, shop and game pictograms come from a curated `Pictogram` set drawn flat in the accent colours with Ink outlines; the keyword-to-emoji map in `feature/chores` `ChoreUi.kt` becomes a keyword-to-`Pictogram` map with the same keys, and the shop and games get the same treatment. Seedling's coin jar, sun and stars (`core/ui` `BalanceJar`, `MinutesSun`, `SavingsStars`) are redrawn in the new palette, same semantics strings.

## 5. Bottom navigation (new in M9, D-M9-6 revised)

Sprout 1.0 navigates with buttons: the parent dashboard links out to chores, approvals, shop and settings, kid screens go wallet → chores / shop / games and back. Kiril asked for a bottom bar as part of the redesign. The app already has a Navigation 3 graph (`app/navigation/SproutApp.kt`: `NavDisplay`, a `NavBackStack<NavKey>`, typed keys in `NavKeys.kt`, `ListDetailSceneStrategy` for Expanded windows), so the bar is a shell around that graph, not a new navigation library.

### 5.1 Destinations

| Shell | Items (left to right) | Keys | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent | Home, Chores, Chat, Settings | `ParentHomeKey`, `ChoresKey`, `ChatThreadsKey`, `SettingsKey` | Approvals, the shop, a kid's wallet, devices, security and AI settings stay pushed screens inside their tab |
| Kid, Sprout and Sapling | Wallet, Chores, Shop, Games, Chat | `KidHomeKey`, `KidChoresKey`, `ShopKey`, `KidGamesKey`, `ChatThreadKey` | Time bank stays reachable from the wallet's screen-time badge |
| Kid, Seedling | Wallet, Chores, Shop | same keys | Three items at 72 dp targets with pictogram icons; games and chat keep their giant buttons on the wallet |

The renders' bars (Wishlist, Family, Badges, Savings) are Stitch's inventions and are ignored; the destinations above are the screens that exist. Tier switches happen in kid mode only; the parent shell has no tier.

### 5.2 Behaviour

- One back stack per destination (`TopLevelBackStack` as in the Navigation 3 samples): switching tabs keeps each tab's pushed screens; the system back button pops within the tab, then returns to the first tab, then leaves the mode. D-M9-11.
- Top-level screens lose the back arrow in `SproutTopBar`; pushed screens keep it. The parent dashboard's duplicate link row (`ParentHomeLinks`) goes; its attention cards and kid cards stay.
- The bar is hidden while the parent lock (`feature/lock`) covers the content, during onboarding, and inside a running game (`GamePlayKey`) and the time bank's running timer so a child cannot tab away mid-session without stopping. D-M9-10.
- Expanded windows (tablets, the list-detail flows of M7) show a `NavigationRail` on the left instead; Material's `NavigationSuiteScaffold` picks bar or rail from the window class. D-M9-12.
- Deep links and the Glance widget target keys as today; the shell selects the tab that owns the key.
- Accessibility: every item has a label (always visible), the selected item is announced as selected, the Seedling bar meets 72 dp, the others 48 dp (56 dp on Sprout); the bar is a traversal group after the content.

### 5.3 Tests and goldens

The bar is drawn by the shell in `app`, so feature-module goldens do not change for it. New screenshot tests in `app` capture the parent shell on each tab (light and dark) and the kid shell per tier, with `checkAccessibility = true`; `SproutNavigationBar` gets per-tier component goldens in `core/designsystem`. The eight showcase images in `tools/store/listing.yaml` are repointed to shell goldens so the Play listing shows the bar; `compose.py` is unchanged. A Robolectric test covers back-stack-per-tab behaviour (push in tab A, switch to B, back pops B, then returns to A with its stack intact).
