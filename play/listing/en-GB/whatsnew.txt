Set up your shop. The dashboard's Rewards button now opens a list of your rewards: add one, give it a price, choose whether it is screen time, a treat or an outing, and offer it to all your kids or only some. Switch "Show in the shop" off to hide a reward without losing it. "See the shop as a kid" shows what each child sees.

Earlier builds had no way to add rewards, so the shop stayed empty.
